**1. Tom Kha Gai (Thai Coconut Galangal Chicken Soup)**  
A fragrant Thai staple that marries creamy coconut milk with the citrusy, floral notes of galangal, lemongrass, and kaffir lime leaves, finished with a bright squeeze of lime and a subtle heat from fresh chilies.  

- 1 liter chicken broth  
- 1 can (400ml) coconut milk  
- 2 stalks lemongrass, bruised  
- 3 slices galangal  
- 2 kaffir lime leaves, torn  
- 300g boneless chicken thigh, sliced  
- 1–2 Thai bird’s eye chilies, sliced  
- 2 tbsp fish sauce  
- 1 tbsp lime juice  
- Fresh cilantro and sliced red chili for garnish  

- Simmer broth, lemongrass, galangal, and kaffir lime leaves for 5 minutes to extract aromatics.  
- Add chicken and cook until opaque, then stir in coconut milk and chilies.  
- Season with fish sauce, finish with lime juice just before serving to preserve freshness.  
- Ladle into bowls, garnish with cilantro and sliced chili, and serve hot with jasmine rice or alone.  

**2. Miso Shiru (Japanese Miso Soup)**  
A light, umami-rich broth that’s a cornerstone of Japanese meals, built on delicate dashi and enriched with fermented miso paste, silky tofu, and the oceanic touch of nori and green onions.  

- 4 cups dashi stock (or water infused with kombu and bonito flakes)  
- 3 tbsp white miso paste *(never boil miso to keep its flavor intact)*  
- 100g silken tofu, cubed  
- 1 sheet nori, cut into thin strips  
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced  
- Optional: ½ tsp soy sauce or a drop of sesame oil  

- Gently warm dashi to about 80°C; avoid a rolling boil.  
- Mix miso with a ladle of warm dashi in a small bowl until smooth, then stir back into the pot.  
- Add tofu cubes and heat gently for 1–2 minutes until warmed through.  
- Top with nori strips and sliced green onions, drizzle with sesame oil if desired, and serve immediately in small bowls.  

**3. Pho Bo (Vietnamese Beef Noodle Soup)**  
Vietnam’s iconic aromatic soup, built on a broth simmered for hours with charred ginger, cinnamon, star anise, and rock sugar, yielding a complex, fragrant base that cradles tender beef slices, rice noodles, and a vibrant array of fresh herbs.  

- 2kg beef bones (marrow and knuckle) + 500g beef flank or brisket  
- 1 large onion, charred over an open flame  
- 2-inch piece ginger, charred  
- 4 star anise, 1 cinnamon stick, 3 cardamom pods  
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed  
- 2 tbsp rock sugar or brown sugar  
- Fish sauce to taste  
- 300g flat rice noodles (bánh pho)  
- Fresh Thai basil, bean sprouts, lime wedges, and sliced chilies for serving  

- Roast onion and ginger over direct flame until blackened, then cover with fresh water in a large pot; bring to a rapid boil and discard the first water to cleanse the stock.  
- Add fresh water, charred aromatics, star anise, cinnamon, cardamom, garlic, and rock sugar; simmer gently for 4–6 hours, skimming any impurities that rise to the surface.  
- Season with fish sauce toward the end, then strain the broth through a fine mesh or cheesecloth.  
- Cook rice noodles per package directions, divide into bowls, and ladle hot broth over them.  
- Top with thinly sliced raw beef (it will cook in the hot broth), bean sprouts, basil, lime, and chilies; serve with hoisin and sriracha on the side for dipping.
